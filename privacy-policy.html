Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026

Sixbee: E6B Flight Computer ("the app") is published by Enes Furkan Bayram. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app does not collect any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. We do not operate servers that receive your data.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, aircraft profiles, flight plans and practice statistics are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is enabled on your device and sync is turned on in the app, your aircraft profiles and flight plans are stored in your personal iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. This data is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot access it. You can turn sync off in Settings ▸ Sync.

Location

The app asks for your location only when you tap "Use my location" or "Variation here". The position is used on the device to fill in coordinates or to compute the local magnetic variation. It is not stored by us and is not sent anywhere.

Weather reports and airports

The airport database is stored inside the app and works offline. If you choose to fetch a live METAR, the app sends only the airport code you typed to the public Aviation Weather Center service (aviationweather.gov) and shows the report it returns. No identifier, account or location is sent with the request, and we do not receive it.

Notifications

Reminders are optional, are scheduled on the device and can be turned off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
