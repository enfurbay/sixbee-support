Support

Updated 3 October 2026

Contact

Questions, a result that looks wrong, or a feature request: write to enfurbay@gmail.com. Please include the calculator name, the values you entered and the result you expected. Replies usually arrive within two working days.

Getting started

  • Calculate. Pick a calculator, type your values and the results update as you type. Tap a unit to change it. Long-press a result to copy it.
  • How it's solved. Every calculator lists the formula, the substituted numbers and the result of each step.
  • Units. Settings ▸ Units sets the defaults for your region. Any field can use a different unit.
  • Airports and METAR. Type an ICAO code to fill runway headings, elevation and coordinates from the built-in airport database. Fetch the live METAR, or paste one, to fill wind, temperature and pressure.
  • Weight and balance. Edit a sample aircraft or add your own with the empty weight, arms and envelope from your aircraft's weight and balance record.
  • Flight plan. Add legs with course, distance, TAS and wind. Open the cockpit card in flight, or export the navigation log as PDF or CSV.

Frequently asked

My density altitude differs slightly from another E6B. Sixbee uses the exact standard-atmosphere formula and also shows the 120 ft per °C rule of thumb used by many slide rules. Small differences between the two are expected.

Does it work without a connection? Yes. Every calculation and the airport database run on the device. Only fetching a live METAR needs a connection.

How does sync work? Aircraft profiles and flight plans sync through your own iCloud account when iCloud is enabled on the device. You can turn it off in Settings ▸ Sync.

Is there a subscription? No. Sixbee is a one-time purchase.

Safety

Sixbee is a training and flight planning aid, not a certified navigation or performance system. Always verify results against your Pilot's Operating Handbook, approved charts and official sources before flight.
