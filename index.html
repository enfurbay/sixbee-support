Sixbee: E6B Flight Computer

For iPhone and iPad

Sixbee is a complete E6B flight computer for student pilots, instructors and everyday flying. Type the numbers you have and the answer appears instantly, together with every formula and step that leads to it.

What it does

  • 25 calculators: wind triangle, crosswind, time, speed and distance, fuel, pressure and density altitude, true airspeed, descent, climb, glide, holding entry and more
  • Step-by-step solutions and animated diagrams on every calculator
  • Weight and balance with CG envelope chart, take-off and landing distance from your POH tables
  • Multi-leg flight plans, a cockpit card for the flight, PDF and CSV export
  • Practice mode with checkride-style questions
  • Works offline. One-time purchase, no subscription, no ads, no account

Get Sixbee on the App Store

Important

Sixbee is a training and flight planning aid. It is not a certified navigation or performance system. Always verify results against your Pilot's Operating Handbook, approved charts and official sources before flight.
